In this project, I analyzed a bank customer churn dataset using MySQL Server, focusing on data cleaning, preparation, and exploratory analysis. The goal was to enhance data quality, identify trends, and uncover key factors contributing to churn. The analysis revealed that older customers, inactive members, and those with low credit scores and balances were more likely to leave the bank. Based on these insights, strategies such as targeted retention efforts, product bundling, and customer re-engagement campaigns can be recommended to reduce churn and improve customer retention.
